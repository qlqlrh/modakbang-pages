Chargin gets you to put your phone down at the bedtime you set. It does not track your sleep.

  • It counts down to your bedtimeSet when you wake up and how long you want to sleep. Your bedtime follows, and the countdown stays on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
  • Two hours before bed, the apps you pick get a gateTap a locked app and you get a question instead. Write what you're going in for and it opens — ten minutes per task, then it closes again.
  • Plug in, and tonight is overThe moment you connect your charger or tap "Going to sleep" is recorded.
  • In the morning, just the factsHow many minutes late you were, and what share of your planned night you protected. We can't know how well you slept, and we don't pretend to.
  • Chargin ProLocks every app from bedtime until you wake. With Strict Mode on, you can't postpone or end that night's plan.
