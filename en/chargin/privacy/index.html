Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026 · Operated by Modakbang

Modakbang ("we") operates the Chargin app ("the app"). This policy describes what the app actually stores and sends. If that changes, this page changes with it.

2. What we do not collect

  • Per-app Screen Time usage. The "apps you used most at night" list is drawn on the device by an iOS system extension. Neither the app nor our server receives those numbers.
  • Usage of apps you open without going through a gate.
  • Whether or how well you slept. The app does not measure sleep.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location.
  • Payment card details. Apple processes payments; the app only checks on the device whether a subscription is active.

3. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data until you delete your account.
  • Tap Settings › Account › Delete account in the app to delete your account and all records above immediately. This cannot be undone.
  • Step events tied to the random install ID remain only as statistics, no longer linked to any account. They cannot identify you on their own.
  • If you delete the app without linking an Apple account, you can no longer reach those records from the app. Email us to have them deleted from the server.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

4. Ads

Free users may see Google AdMob ads. Ads appear only outside the night, such as on the morning record screen, and only when you choose to watch one.

  • To show an ad, Google processes device and app information, an approximate region inferred from your IP address, and ad impression and click data through its SDK.
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for personalized ads only if you allow tracking in the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt. Otherwise you see non-personalized ads. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • We do not receive this data, and we never use your night or gate records for advertising.
  • Pro subscribers see no ads.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information at any time. You can edit your name or delete your account in the app; for anything else, email us and we will respond without delay. If you are in California or the EU/UK, you have the rights granted by your local law, and we honor them through the same channel.

7. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Every server table is protected by row-level access control, so each user can read and write only their own records.
  • Values you should not change, such as subscription status, cannot be written from the app.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 (under 13 in the US), and we do not knowingly collect their information.
